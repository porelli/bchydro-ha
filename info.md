Monitor your BC Hydro electricity consumption and costs in Home Assistant.

## Features

- Real-time consumption tracking
- Cost monitoring
- Billing information and estimates
- Daily breakdown data
- Energy Dashboard integration with hourly statistics
- Automatic session management

## Sensors

- Consumption to Date (kWh)
- Cost to Date ($)
- Estimated Consumption (kWh)
- Estimated Cost ($)
- Yesterday's Consumption (kWh)
- Yesterday's Cost ($)
- Rate Plan
- Billing Period info

## Requirements

- BC Hydro account with smart meter
- Home Assistant 2024.1.0 or newer
